akh is a violent package of 4 custom distortion algorithms engineered to wreak unbridled destruction on a signal. the four algorithms include "root," an algorithm that interacts more with higher harmonics; "euler," a warm hyperbolic tangent style transfer function; "absolute," an abrasive, lossy algorithm that rips sounds to shreds; and "sqrtube," an algorithm inspired by the asymmetry of tube distortions.

terr is a versatile ring modulator based on wave terrain synthesis, a synthesis technique similar to wavetable synthesis. the unique harmonic characteristics created through this synthesis method allow for a ring modulator that doesn't completely hijack the harmonic content of a signal. instead, terr can act as a gentle timbral tool.
