griffin storey

Griffin Storey is an audio engineer and professional musician based in Boston, MA and originally hailing from Seattle, WA. He plays and writes music for Still Life, performs as a hired gun, and records, mixes and masters records. After graduating from Berklee College of Music with a degree in music production, Griffin apprenticed with Benny Grotto at Mad Oak Studios and Sean McLaughlin at 37’ Productions.

Griffin approaches every project with the artist’s vision at the forefront. He brings an open mind and an intent to learn and understand, as to best translate that vision and emotion into recorded music. Using his versatile set of skills, he is proficient in most genres and styles, and is always looking to bring something fresh and unique to every project. He serves the music, with consistent preparation, punctuality, adaptability and work ethic. Most of all, he thrives on feedback and conversation, as communication is paramount in truly bringing a vision to life.

Take a look around this site to take a listen to Griffin’s work, and what he can offer you. For all inquiries, reach out through the contact sheet.
